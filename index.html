Nutrition and Diet Guide

Safe vs. Toxic Foods

Always check what you feed your pets. Some human foods are healthy, while others are very dangerous.

Safe Foods for Pets

  • Plain boiled chicken (no bones or salt)
  • Carrots and cucumber slices
  • Cooked pumpkin and plain rice
  • Seedless apple slices and bananas
  • Clean, fresh drinking water

Toxic Foods to Avoid

  • Chocolate and coffee
  • Grapes and raisins
  • Onions, garlic, and chives
  • Cooked bones (can splinter)
  • Candies with Xylitol

Basic Grooming and Hygiene

Bathing and Brushing Tips

Regular grooming keeps your pet's coat clean and helps you check for ticks and fleas.

  • Brush short-haired pets weekly and long-haired pets every other day.
  • Bathe dogs every few weeks using mild pet shampoo.
  • Dry your pet properly with a clean towel after bathing.

Nail, Ear and Dental Care

  • Nails: Trim only the clear tips of the nails once a month.
  • Ears: Gently wipe the outer ears with a damp cotton pad weekly.
  • Teeth: Brush teeth regularly using pet toothpaste.

Health Tracker and Vaccination Check

Vaccine Check Schedule

Select your pet's current vaccination stage:

  • Puppy or Kitten Stage: First Core Vaccine and Deworming
  • Follow-up Visit: Second Booster Shot and Deworming
  • Young Adult Stage: Final Booster and Anti-Rabies Vaccine
  • Yearly Routine: Annual Booster and Vet Checkup

Critical Signs of a Pet Health Emergency

Bring your pet to the vet right away if you see these signs:

  • Difficulty breathing or pale gums
  • Repeated vomiting or bloody diarrhea
  • Sudden collapse, seizures, or weakness
  • Swollen, hard, or painful stomach
  • Eating chocolate, rat poison, or toxic foods
